Set up the account (10 minutes)

  1. Download TikTok and sign up. Username ideas: @kurdishtank, @tanki.kurdi, @kurdish.tank.game.
  2. Use the profile picture from the Pictures tab.
  3. Go to Settings → Account → Switch to Business Account (free). This lets you put your game link in your bio straight away. Personal accounts need about 1,000 followers first.
  4. Paste one of the bios below, and put your game link (your Render address) in the Website field.

10 video ideas (short, 10–25 seconds)

  1. “Kurdistan now has its own tank game” — fast clip driving round the Citadel.
  2. “Can you drive around Hawler Citadel?” — one lap of the ring road.
  3. “1 shot = 1 tank” — grab the red one-shot power-up and use it.
  4. Me vs my friends — a funny moment from a real match with friends.
  5. “Which map is best?” — Hawler, desert, forest; ask people to vote in the comments.
  6. Shield saves me — take hits with the blue shield, then win.
  7. “Made by a Kurd” — tell the story of making the game (people love this).
  8. Speed run in Shar Park — speed boost through the park and fountain.
  9. Language switch — show the game in Kurdish, Arabic, then English.
  10. Challenge — “Beat me in my room: code F7K2Q”, then post the results.

How to record and post

  • Record with your phone's screen recorder while playing (landscape), then crop to vertical in TikTok or CapCut.
  • First second matters most: start with an explosion or a kill, not the menu.
  • Add big text in Kurdish on the video, and use a trending sound.
  • Post once a day for 2 weeks, in the evening (about 7–10 pm Kurdistan time).
  • Reply to comments with a new video. It's the fastest way to grow.
  • End every video with “link in bio — free, no download”.

How to tell if people like it

  • In TikTok: Views, Profile views and Website clicks (Business account analytics).
  • On your server: open your-link/stats to see how many rooms are being played right now.
  • Good early signs: people asking for the link, tagging friends, or asking for new maps.
